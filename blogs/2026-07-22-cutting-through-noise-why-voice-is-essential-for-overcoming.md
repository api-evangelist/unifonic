In today’s hyperconnected world, an average person is inundated with numerous notifications, such as emails and SMS alerts, all competing for attention. Ironically, the very systems designed to keep us informed are now being ignored. This growing ‘notification fatigue’ presents a critical challenge, particularly in emergencies where every second counts and missed messages can have serious consequences.
